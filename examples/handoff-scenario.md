# Illustrative scenario — Arsenal / Gillespie Road

> **Status: demonstration only.** Arsenal/Gillespie Road appears among the research plugin's documented acceptance-test scenarios. The example below **is not a transcript of an executed plugin run**, a verified history article, or proof of a published Manto12FC post. No historical claim is asserted as verified here.

The scenario shows **how the dossier and human handoff are meant to work**, using a railway-station naming story as the topic.

## Human topic selection

- **Working subject:** How the station associated with Arsenal received its club-related name.
- **Requested research:** dates, historical names, club context, period documents, and image sources.
- **Editorial goal:** prepare a historically traceable story suitable for an Instagram carousel.

The research agent should not label any of these facts confirmed until it has actually consulted relevant sources and documented evidence. In a full dossier, unresolved chronological differences and usage rights must appear explicitly.

## Manual transfer checkpoint

The human editor:

1. Reads the completed research dossier and checks the references.
2. Selects which claims and images can responsibly be used.
3. Passes the relevant dossier text/attachment **manually** to the separate Content Editor plugin.
4. Carries forward any caveats, unverified claims, and image-rights restrictions.

## Agent 02 — expected transformation

If the underlying dossier is actually verified, the Content Editor can choose a narrative arc, draft carousel text and an accompanying caption, associate specific documented `Vxx` candidates with relevant cards, and flag any fact requiring one more check. The number of cards and story opening should follow available evidence, not a universal template.

**There is no sample final article here:** fabricating a completed response would misrepresent an instructional scenario as a real result.

## Human Canva composition — expected stage

After Agent 02, the creator would manually insert the researched text and authorized imagery into a Manto12FC Canva template, adjusting hierarchy, sizing, cropping and visual rhythm across slides. This illustrative scenario does **not** contain an actual Canva file, a finished carousel, or proof of publication.
