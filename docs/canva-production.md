# Manual Canva production — Manto12FC

## Position in the workflow

The second plugin (**Content Editor**) delivers texts and, when available, image materials, previews, and/or source-linked visual references. **The creator manually applies these outputs to an existing Manto12FC template in Canva.** The plugins do not access a Canva workspace, edit a Canva design, or export final graphics automatically.

## What the creator does

1. Select the relevant card copy, caption or script text produced by the Content Editor.
2. Select usable imagery from the second-agent output or obtain the image directly from its documented source; resolve provenance, credit and usage rights.
3. Open the appropriate existing Manto12FC Canva template.
4. Place the text and images manually into the layout; adjust line breaks, font hierarchy, alignment, image crop, contrast and composition.
5. Inspect the resulting slides/static post for accurate text, correct images, readable mobile layout, and consistent visual identity.
6. Export the design and submit it to human editorial review before publishing.

## Why this matters

The AI-assisted process accelerates research and drafting while the **visual direction and composition remain human design work**. The actual handoffs are: research dossier → content text / imagery → manual Canva design → reviewed artwork. No automated Canva integration or template-filling system is claimed.

## Rights and publication

Images shown or suggested by a plugin are not automatically licensed for reuse. Preserve authorship, source records, credit requirements and explicit permissions before inclusion in a public post. Final publication remains a human decision.
