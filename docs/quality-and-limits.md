# Quality principles, limits and scope

## What is implemented in the documented plugins

- Separate configurations for research and production.
- A structured dossier contract to transfer evidence and visual references.
- Editorial guidance for research, narrative choice, carousels, videos and captions.
- Manual assembly of Agent 02 outputs inside existing Canva templates, followed by human review as a required publishing control.

## What is NOT claimed

- Automatic agent-to-agent synchronization or triggers.
- Programmatic orchestration or API integration between plugins.
- Guaranteed or continuous background fact-checking of breaking news.
- Automatic creation of production-ready graphic design files or Canva template population.
- Direct-to-Instagram publishing or autonomous post scheduling.
- Automatically cleared copyright/usage rights for researched photography.
- Benchmark scores, accuracy guarantees, or executed acceptance tests.

## Practical review checklist

- Verify names, dates, teams, seasons, and chronology against consulted sources.
- Review strong claims and source-specific evidence identifiers (`Fxx`).
- Carry forward contested, uncertain, or outdated status rather than smoothing it away.
- Ensure photos/documents are correctly identified and have appropriate credits and permission (`Vxx`).
- Check the manually composed Canva artwork for cropping, layout fidelity, text overflow, legibility, and template consistency.
- Confirm the narrative says something new on every slide or scene.
- Treat final approval as a human editorial decision.

## Publication and privacy decisions

This public repository deliberately provides **documentation rather than private plugin exports**. It excludes internal prompt/configuration files, full reference corpora, private scheduling and planning datasets, third-party video, photograph reproductions, and reference images of uncertain redistribution rights.

## Interpretation

“AI agent” here means a **purpose-built ChatGPT plugin with a defined role, instructions, and workflow skills**. “Multi-agent editorial workflow” means a **two-stage human-supervised process**; it does not claim autonomous software communication.
